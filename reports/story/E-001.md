## Step 1: B100-V2 (3.0 min Deep-Dive floor)

The rule as applied (written down before any metric was computed):
- Dive length is speech minutes, the same measure as the B100 placement table. That table has 11 of 37 dives under 3.0 min.
- Dives are taken in EDL order. A dive under 3.0 min merges into the adjacent dive of the same part: the next one if it exists, otherwise the previous one. The host keeps its anchor, and the segments stay in source order, so nothing within a part is reordered.
- A dive with no same-part neighbour moves to the part's next home chapter. This never happened.
- The process repeats until every dive is 3.0 min or longer.

Merges in both directions caused violations, so neither 'always next' nor 'always previous' gives 0. No new previews were recorded for these 5: that would be a new review, outside the E-001 rules.

## Step 1: B100-cap-V2 (3:30:00 cap)

- Start from B100-V2. Rank every Deep-Dive segment (a contiguous EDL cut) by distinct novel idea units per minute. Restatement sentences count 0. Minutes include the lead gap.
- Ties break by EDL position.
- Drop segments in ascending order. Skip a segment if dropping it adds any (concept, prerequisite) pair to the violation or never-mentioned sets: that means a kept later sentence needs it.
- Stop at runtime <= 3:30:00. A dive left with no segments loses its cards. This never happened.

## Pre-registered rules evaluated on steps 1-2 (the critic coherence reads, step 3, are still pending)

- **Q2**: V2 needs B100-V2 to have 0 violations, 100.0 % coverage, and coherence not 1.0 or more below 6.25. It has 5 violations and 100.0 % coverage, so the violation condition fails and **the rule gives V1** whatever the coherence result.

## Caveats

- The neighbour choice ('next if any, else previous; host keeps its anchor') is the story-editor's literal reading of 'merge into an adjacent dive'. It was fixed before measuring. Moving the merged dive to the later anchor instead would change which sentences cross which trunk chapters. It was not run, because only one rule was allowed.
- Novelty is an upper bound (P4 audit: about 8.4 %, range 3.7-19.3 %, of novel sentences may be restatements), so 'novel units/min' in the cap ranking inherits that noise.
- Coherence for B100-V2 and B100-cap-V2 is not scored here. Authors do not grade their own work, so that is step 3 (critic).

4. **The CH-04 promise.** The line "Airflow in 35 minutes" is wrong under this EDL; the real gap is about 115 min.
