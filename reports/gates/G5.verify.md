## Follow-ups (none blocks G5)

- **F1. Audible speech with no word-map entries (9 spans, 32.06 s, 0.23 % of runtime).**
  - The author's radio-check lists these as "word-map holes over audible untranscribed speech". They are not silent: median level is -14.7 to -25.6 dBFS in every span (measured by me).
  - Spans: DD-P02-1 at 1,217,260 ms; DD-P13 at 7,344,700 and 7,421,030; DD-P14 at 7,878,870; DD-P19-1 at 9,908,610 (5.35 s, an agenda); DD-P17 at 10,552,830, 10,575,330 and 10,757,820; DD-P20 at 11,168,310.
  - Contents: section headings ("القسم الثالث معمارية FastAPI" and similar), an agenda, and a spoken number. The DD-P17 span at 10,757,820 follows the token `125,847`, which was aligned to a very short span, so the number's end time in the map is probably early.
  - Two Whisper strings in the radio-check are hallucinated subtitle boilerplate ("ترجمة نانسي قنقر", "اشتركوا في القناة") laid over real loud audio.
  - This does not violate any listed G5 criterion: the gate's gap rule is acoustic, and every EDL sentence word is mapped.
  - It matters for golden rule 2 and for "every spoken number appears on screen". A picture anchor cannot point at speech with no word id.
  - **Recommendation:** re-transcribe the nine spans, add their words, and re-derive word map v1.1. The VO and the existing ids stay unchanged. This should be done before P8, because the lock rule says any change bumps the version.
- **F2. Coverage leans on a self-audit.** 9 units count as included only through audit-confirmed restatements. The audit (`reports/graph/novelty_audit.json`) says "author self-audit; independent re-verification pending". Without those 9, 9 units would be neither included nor waived. The units are iu:00803, 00907, 01109, 01463, 01657, 02389, 02487, 02513 and 02680. I read each pair, with its context sentences, in Arabic. 8 are clear restatements of the paired S1 sentence. iu:01463 (`s:S4:P09:0055`, "it is excellent at dedup") is borderline. It is not a clear restatement of `s:S1:ar-natural:0184`, but the same dedup claim (ROW_NUMBER is the dedup tool) is carried by two included sentences, `s:S4:P09:0049` and S1:0184. I accept it as covered. If the lead wants a stricter reading, the fix is to include that 3 s sentence, which means an EDL change and a re-lock. 00803 is the P01 broadcast-join unit, restated by S1:0388.
- **F3. The 0 violations depends on 40 B-context review rows.** With only the global (context-free) review rows the same order has 21 violations; with previews and removed mentions also disabled it has 57. All 223 review rows were written by `story-editor`, the same role that built the EDL. I read all 40 B-context rows and 16 random global rows. They are reasonable: passing mentions, agenda lines, same-deep-dive explanations, and homonym false positives such as `2>/dev/null` being read as SQL NULL. I found none I would overturn. The gate remains numeric, but this independent read is the only separation of author and reviewer for these rows.
- **F4. Runtime is 3:51:00, not 4:01:51.** The difference of 10:51 is the radio edit: 306 pauses tightened (486.9 s) plus block-edge trimming. It is within W-001-RT (cap 4:01:51). It is still above the plan's 3.5 h target and relies on that waiver.
- **F5. Word-map onset accuracy.** 5 of 324 re-aligned words differ from the map by more than 150 ms. The largest is `w:S4:P17:000334` (1,150 ms, MMS confidence 0.22), in the DD-P17 area that also holds F1 spans. The sync-verifier should check DD-P17 first.
