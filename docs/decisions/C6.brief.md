# C6 brief: P7 component and engine change requests

Chair: council-chair, 2026-10-30. **Brief only**: neutral, no recommendation. Step 1 of 3 (brief, ballot, ADR).

Scope: `corpus/specs/_component_requests.jsonl` holds 19 lines and 17 ids. Excluded: CR-001/002 (resolved, ADR-011) and CR-011/012 (engine owner). In scope, all `open`: CR-003…010 and CR-013…017. No other CR is open.

## Shared facts
- **Freeze** (`harness/state/freeze.json`): the hashed files are the tokens, type, fonts, 106 `schema.ts` and 106 `harness/schemas/components/*.json`. `studio/src/spec/*` and `studio/scripts/p7.ts` are not hashed.
- **Timing guard** (ADR-011, AT-13R2 ruling): any timing-engine change re-runs the affected 24 fps solo layers from a pinned engine.
- **Budgets** (ADR-002), in box s/frame: lite and standard 0.201, hero 1.038.
  - Hero is capped at 5 % of runtime (RT5).
  - R2 fires above a blended 0.240.
  - R5 (FX2 to FX3) fires if standard exceeds 0.201.
- **Baselines**: 91 sets, 60 of them `approved-critic`. A perf run takes about 47 s wall (DepthLayers: 3 tiers, 96 frames).
- **G6b** requires every component to be implemented or explicitly mapped/deferred. `reports/gates/G6b.json` (today) reads "not implemented yet": the check itself is missing.
- **P8**:
  - Only frozen components may be used (04 §8), and no chapter spec exists yet.
  - The 73 packs (`corpus/packs/*.json`) offer components and their props.
  - The pilot packs CH-33 and DD-P23 list VectorGalaxy, ContextTunnel, MatchCut and CameraRig; CH-33 also lists RowFunnel.

**Options:**
- **A**: accept as proposed.
- **M**: accept-modified, as named in the row.
- **D**: defer to the named phase. P8 uses the current workaround meanwhile.
- **R**: reject. The workaround becomes permanent and is documented in `docs/tools/component_guide.md`.

**Consequences:**
- A or M on a schema CR (Q4–Q11) is a contract change: ADR, re-freeze, then `dc gate check G6a`. All accepted schema CRs can share one batch.
  - Approved baselines stay unchanged if the demo omits the new prop (verify 0 px). Otherwise the component is re-baselined and goes to the critic.
- A or M on an engine or tooling CR (Q1–Q3, Q12, Q13) leaves the hashed files unchanged. Effects on baselines are given per row.
- D and R change nothing now and leave the limitation recorded for G6b.
  - A deferred schema change that lands during P8 also re-lints the specs that use the component.

## Criteria (weights sum to 1.0)
- C1 Correctness against 04 §1.3/§2/§6/§8 and the data contract (no invented or misstated number): **0.30**
- C2 P8 expressiveness (can a scene-director express the teaching beat?): **0.25**
- C3 Risk to the frozen contract and approved baselines: **0.25**
- C4 Cost and schedule (compute, re-tests, the G6b-to-G8 path): **0.20**

## Questions

**Q1, CR-003: transition props, MatchCut geometry, hero motion blur.** Evidence: the CR, `docs/p7/catalog_status/fx-b.md`, `reports/p7/review_fx-b.md` B5.
- Props: `resolve.ts` keeps only `{type, frames}`. `direction`, `color`, `motion_blur` and MatchCut `from/to/kind` are validated, then dropped.
- Blur: a whole-frame CSS blur (whip 24 px, streak 12 px) runs at every tier. 04 §1.3 allows none at lite and standard, and `CameraMotionBlur` on hero whips only.
- Status: all three transitions are **mapped** and cannot be mounted. The critic will not approve them until CR-003 is implemented.
- Drop-in: `_fx-b/transitions.ts` passes 69 unit checks.
- Packs: MatchCut is in 73 of 73, the other two in 0.
- Baselines: single-shot demos end on a 0-frame transition, so no existing baseline should contain one (verify).
- Cost: `CameraMotionBlur` is unmeasured.

Options:
- **A:** the engine owner wires everything. Re-tests:
  - the resolve, p7 and shot-level checks;
  - an onset check on one whip boundary and one match boundary;
  - the timing guard;
  - a two-shot demo and its strips;
  - the critic.
- **M:** wire direction, colour, no blur below hero, and the cross-cut streak now. MatchCut geometry and the hero blur wait for P9 and a perf run.
- **D (P9) / R:** always RTL; always `C.signal`; whole-frame blur; a blind 1.04 scale.
- **Experiment:** one 10 s two-shot render at standard and hero, with perf.

**Q2, CR-004: CameraRig `follow`.**

- Critic condition: no scene may use `follow` until it lands, and spec lint should warn.
- CameraRig is approved at 7.

Options:
- **A:** the engine owner resolves the target centre inside the truck envelope. Re-tests: the envelope checks and the timing guard.
- **D (P9):** keeps truck behaviour, plus a lint warning.
- **R:** truck behaviour permanently, and lint rejects `follow`.

**Q3, CR-005: `DemoDef.features`.**

- AudioReactive is approved at 7.0, on condition that the real-P5 strip is the proof. It is in 0 packs.

Options:

- **M:** one `DemoDef` extension for both features and a second shot, which Q1's demos need.
- **D (P9) / R:** the strip remains the proof.

**Q4, CR-006: ThresholdSlider `zones[].ref`.**

- The component has no baseline status, no perf report and no catalog-status row. It is in 3 packs.

Options:

- **M:** no schema change. Lint accepts the shot's `data_refs` (05 §8), unbound to a zone.

**Q5, CR-007: RowFunnel stages, cap 8 to 9.**

- **M:** cap 9, with the demo kept at 8 stages.
- **D (P9) / R:** nine stages cannot be drawn.

**Q6, CR-008: JoinFusion row data.**
- The demo uses 5 + 5 abstract keys.
- `d:6.4:1` is 6 customers in, 15 rows out (Amara 4, Ravi 3, Lena 4, Yuki 2, Tom and Sara 1 null each).
- Chair arithmetic: that is 13 order rows, against the proposed cap of 8 per table.
- JoinFusion is approved at 7.6 and is in 4 packs.

Options:
- **A:** `rows: [{key}]`, capped at 8.
- **M:** a multiset that fits `d:6.4:1`, such as `{key, count}` or a cap of at least 13.
- **D (P9) / R:** a generic match structure only.

**Q10, CR-014: VectorGalaxy scores.**

**Q11, CR-015: ContextTunnel per-item `tokens`.**

**Q12, CR-016: snapshot frame `a1`.**
- The rule is `a1 = first anchor + longest reveal + 1`. It falls before the beat for:
  - FixSettle (anchor f38, a1 f11);
  - Pulse (a1 f13, beat f38–53);
  - FailureGlitch and PathDraw.
- All four are approved, with accepted beat strips.
- A global rule change would move up to 91 stills, 60 of them approved.

Options:
- **A:** an optional `snap_frames`. Components that opt in are re-baselined and go to the critic.
- **M:** an optional fifth `beat` still, with the four existing stills kept. The critic asked for "a standard-tier a1 at f39".
- **D (P9) / R:** the strips stay the evidence.

**Q13, CR-017: depth of field at standard.**
- Measurements at standard, 1080p, 3 slots (in the CR text only, not in a perf JSON):
  - 0.194 with no blurred plane;
  - 0.311 with one;
  - 0.485 with two;
  - budget 0.201.
- Hero measures 0.478 against 1.038.
- The shipped demo uses blur 0 (standard 0.186).
- 04 §1.3 says standard DOF is "fake (blurred layers)".
- The approved baselines are lite, where `dofBlurPx` is 0, so they are unaffected.

Options:
- **A:** blur far planes only, at half size. The saving of about 4× is unmeasured. Then perf and a critic still.
- **M:** DOF at hero only (5 % of runtime at most), plus a lint warning on any `planes[].blur > 0` in a standard shot.
- **D (P9):** blur 0, no warning.
- **R:** no DOF at standard.
- **Experiment:** one perf run of the half-size variant (two planes, standard), plus a critic A/B still.

## Ballot
- **Voters:** motion-design, technical-accuracy, producer and educator. The other three lenses do not vote.
- **Per question, each lens returns:**
  - its choice;
  - its confidence (0 to 1);
  - one risk;
  - an experiment.
- **Decision rule:**
  - The option with the most votes wins.
  - A tie for the most votes goes to **D**, which leaves the contract and baselines unchanged. So does a plurality whose confidence-weighted lead is under 5 points.
  - A question with mean confidence below 0.6 returns `needs_experiment`.
- **Publication:** the chair publishes vote shares and confidence-weighted shares, as in ADR-011.
