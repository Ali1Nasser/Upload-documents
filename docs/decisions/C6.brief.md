**Options:**
- **A**: accept as proposed.
- **M**: accept-modified (named in the row).
- **D**: defer to the named phase; P8 uses the current workaround meanwhile.
- **R**: reject; the workaround becomes permanent (documented in `docs/tools/component_guide.md`).

**Consequences:**
- **A/M on a schema CR (Q4–Q11, Q14)** is a contract change: ADR, re-freeze, then `dc gate check G6a`. One batch can cover all of them.
  - Baselines are unchanged if the demo omits the new prop (verify 0 px); otherwise re-baseline and critic.
- **A/M on an engine or tooling CR** (Q1–Q3, Q12, Q13) changes no hash; baseline effects per row.
- **D/R** change nothing now. A deferred schema change landing in P8 also re-lints its specs.

## Criteria (sum 1.0)
- **C1 (0.30)** correctness: fidelity to 04 §1.3/§2/§6/§8 and the data contract.
- **C2 (0.25)** P8 expressiveness: can a spec express the teaching beat?

- `resolve.ts` keeps only `{type, frames}`; `direction`, `color`, `motion_blur` and MatchCut `from/to/kind` are dropped.

- All three are **mapped** (unmountable); the critic will not approve them before CR-003. The drop-in passes 69 unit checks.
- MatchCut is in 73/73 packs. Single-shot demos hold no transition frame (fx-b; verify).
- The cost of `CameraMotionBlur` is unmeasured.

Options:
- **A**: the engine owner wires all of it. Re-tests: unit checks, onset at one whip and one match boundary, the timing guard, two-shot strips, critic.

- **Experiment**: a 10 s two-shot render at standard and hero, with perf.

## Ballot
