Chair: council-chair · 2026-10-09 · Status: **brief only**, neutral, no recommendation. Figures are quoted from the cited files.

## 0 · Questions and fixed context

- **Q1 Spine + runtime:** which candidate EDL becomes the film?
- **Q2 Voice policy:** how are the S4 voices handled? This applies to every option except A.
- **Q3 S2 role:** what is the role of S2 (Esraa, 70:05)? It is recorded in ADR-001.

**Fixed context** (constraints, not criteria):

- **User intent:** one long Egyptian-Arabic film, all content covered by semantic merge, every word illustrated, premium look.
- **Plan targets:**
  - runtime 2.5–3.5 h (`00` §4);
  - D2: 100 % of idea units in the EDL or waived by ADR;
  - G5: redundancy ≤ 5 %, 0 prerequisite violations, radio edit with 0 clipped words, no unplanned gap > 2.5 s.
- **No new speech is synthesised** (`03` P5.3), so re-voicing is not an option.

**Members return** (`02` §3), per Q: choice, 1–10 score per option per criterion, risks, confidence 0–1, and the experiment you would run if unsure. Score *n/m* (not measured) cells under stated assumptions.

**Coherence** (`candidates.md`, "Coherence"):
- Spread ≤ 1.0 point, n = 2 judges per candidate; the report itself says "coherence alone does not decide ADR-001".
- Recurring judge points:
  - (1) An orientation stack of 21–27 min before CH-03 in B, B100, C and D.
  - (2) Trunk-order problems shared by all candidates: CH-15–20 zigzag, CH-17 comes before CH-23.
  - (3) Seven dives restate the trunk: DD-P08a, P08b, P11a, P11b, P15, P22a, P24.

  - (9) The S1 arc and its callbacks are credited.
- Excluded from the means: J6, J7 and J16 (partial input) and J13–J15 (wrong file).

## 2 · Q2 Voice policy (B, B100, B100-cap, C, D)

### Facts

**Voices** (`corpus/audio/voices.json`; ECAPA cosine to S1):
- **Voice A**, 10 parts (P00, P00b, P02, P05, P08, P13, P15, P16, P20, P24): 0.502–0.615.
- **Voice B**, 16 parts (the S3 TTS family): 0.333–0.386.
- **Voice C**, P01 only: 0.274 to S1 and 0.583 to the English S5.
- `reports/audio/sources.md` prints 0.53–0.62 for A and 0.34–0.42 for B. The JSON is quoted here.

**Voice time in B100:** S1 70.1 min, A 62.5, B 104.2, C 8.1.

**Signal** (`sources.md`):
- S4 integrated loudness is −21.9 to −20.4 LUFS, so it needs about +5 dB.
- True peaks reach +1.1 dBTP. Clip runs: P00 = 2, P01 = 21.
- 99 % roll-off is 7300–10078 Hz, against 8602 Hz for S1.
- An EQ match clamped to ±4 dB is ready in `corpus/audio/eq_match_s4_to_s1.json`.

### Options

| id | Policy |
|---|---|
| **V1** | Each S4 part plays in its recorded voice: 2 s entry and exit cards with a sting, speech-gated −16 LUFS, true-peak limiter, EQ match ±4 dB, ≤ 2 switches per trunk chapter. This is the plan default and what B/B100 implement. |
| **V2** | V1 plus a minimum Deep-Dive length of 3.0 min: shorter dives merge with an adjacent dive or move to the next home chapter. In the B100 placement table, 11 of 37 dives are under 3.0 min, and they account for 22 of the 66 switches (derived). The effect on prerequisite order and coherence is n/m. |

### Criteria

Weights (sum 1.00): audible continuity (switches, voice match) **0.35** · placement fidelity and prerequisite order **0.25** · coverage preserved **0.20** · build and QA cost **0.20**.
