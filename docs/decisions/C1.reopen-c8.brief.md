# C1 reopen brief: ADR-001 criterion 8, handling of DD-P01 (English voice C)

Chair: council-chair · 2026-10-09 · Status: **brief only**, neutral, no recommendation. *n/m* = not measured.

## 0 · Question and scope

- **Trigger:** the pre-registered rule in `harness/state/decisions.json` (ADR-001 `experiment.rules.dialect`) reopens criterion 8 when more than 30 % of windows are non-Egyptian. `reports/story/E-001_dialect.md` found **5 of 15 (33 %)**. All 5 are voice C, i.e. S4 P01, which is English speech. Voices A and B: 10 of 10 Egyptian.
- **Question:** how does the film handle block DD-P01?
- **Not reopened:** Q1 = B100 and Q2 = V1, both fixed by the E-001 rules (`reports/story/E-001.md`). Only criterion 8, "Language and accuracy exposure" (`docs/decisions/C1.brief.md` §1), is reopened.
- **User intent (relayed):** "professional Egyptian Arabic video", "every spoken word accompanied by synchronized visuals", "long illustrative video", "all content covered".
- **Plan:**
  - D1: one Egyptian-Arabic film.
  - D2: 100 % of idea units in the EDL, or waived by ADR.
  - D10: soft Arabic subtitles.
  - `03` P5.3: "No new speech is synthesised".
  - `04` §11 *Don't*: bullet lists, paragraphs on screen.

## 1 · Facts

Sources: `reports/story/E-001_noC.md` and `.json`; `corpus/edl/candidates/B100-noC.json`.

- **B100-noC** keeps 73 of B100's 74 blocks byte-equal.
- **P01:** 106 sentences.
  - 6.72 min of English-dominant speech; voice C time 8.1 min.
  - ECAPA match to S1 0.274; 21 clip runs.
- **Dropping DD-P01** loses 103 units (`iu:00735`–`00843`, minus 6).
  - All are novel and in the orientation part; 102 exist only in P01.
  - The drop adds 0 prerequisite violations.
- **Class of the lost units (strict):** 33 topic-covered, 26 partly, **44 truly lost**.
  - The 44: 27 claims, 9 transitions, 4 questions, 2 examples, 2 recaps.
  - The class rests on concept ids, because cross-language cosine never reaches 0.81 (max 0.803).
- **Substantive core,** per the story-editor (unconfirmed): about 10–15 units, roughly 1–1.5 min, some of them in the "partly" class:
  - the 4-step predict/inject/diagnose/fix loop;
  - hash index = exact match only;
  - the 90 % skew example;
  - the weekly pacing;
  - the 3 portfolio projects;
  - the interview questions.
- **Other English:** outside P01 there are 15 English-heavy sentences (0.85 min). All are titles or terms inside Egyptian speech.
- **Arabic sources for P01:** there is no Arabic audio and no Egyptian script of P01.
  - The 28-min V2 narration §01 (977 chars) shares the theme but has none of P01's specifics.
  - Its video `f:ca1d3f72ca14` has never been ASR'd.
- **TTS:** none is installed.
  - MMS-ara is MSA; the story-editor rates it "Not acceptable".
  - The Egyptian models are 2.1–5.4 GB (two of them NC-licensed), untested and CPU-only.
  - About 7 GB of disk is free.

## 4 · What members return

For each option, return:
- a score of 1–10 on each criterion;
- your choice;
- the risks;
- your confidence (0–1);
- the smallest experiment that would change your vote.

Synthesis follows `02` §3. If the top two are within 5 %, or mean confidence is below 0.6, the result is `needs_experiment`.
