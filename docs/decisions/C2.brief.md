# C2 brief: ADR-002 render stack, ADR-003 look and typography, ADR-004 captions, ADR-005 music and SFX

Chair: council-chair · 2026-10-09 · **Brief only**: neutral, no recommendation. Derived figures show their formula.

## 0 · Fixed context (constraints, not criteria)

- **User intent:** one long Egyptian-Arabic film, all content, every word illustrated, premium look. Cost may change *how* something is shown, not *what* is shown (`reports/story/producer_notes.md` item 5).
- **Runtime:** ADR-001 is open, so we plan for **4:02:00–4:10:07** (B100 4:10:07, B100-V2 4:09:31; `reports/story/E-001.md`).
- **Deliverable targets:**
  - D1: 1080p30 ("24 fps only if ADR-002 says so");
  - D9: −16 ± 0.5 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1.0 dBTP;
  - D10: soft Arabic subtitles;
  - G10a: sync median ≤ 40 ms, p95 ≤ 100 ms.
- **G6a** requires ADR-002…005 decided, look ≥ 8.0, parity ≥ 7.5, and a projected render ≤ 24 h (or an ADR on fps, FX share or scale-up). Measured today: look **7.4**, parity **6.9**, lowest single criterion **4**, so G6a **fails** (`reports/lookdev/critic.md`). These ADRs do not waive G6a.
- **Resources:**
  - Box: 4 vCPU, no GPU, 3 slots. A scale-up needs the user; it is not scored here.
  - Tokens are the binding constraint: 4 usage-limit hits, about 14 M subagent tokens so far, 5–8 k tokens per EDL sentence in P8–P9.
- **On-screen numbers and terms** are mandatory and word-anchored, whatever ADR-004 decides.

**Members return**, per question: a choice, 1–10 scores (every option × every criterion), risks, confidence 0–1, and the experiment you would run. Mark unmeasured cells *n/m* and state your assumption.

## 1 · ADR-002 Render stack

### Measured cost (swiftshader, concurrency 3)

| Class (source) | s/frame/slot | box s/frame |
|---|---|---|
| Bench2D: kinetic words + SVG (`reports/perf/render_bench.md`) | 0.304 | 0.101 |
| BenchGlow: 400 CSS glows + backdrop blur | 0.543 | 0.181 |
| BenchR3F: 3,000 instances + Bloom | 3.114 | 1.038 |
| MA kinetic type, standard / CSS-hero (`reports/perf/lookdev.json`) | 0.398 / 0.384 | 0.133 / 0.128 |
| MB galaxy, SVG 1,500 (standard) | 0.606 | 0.202 |
| MB galaxy, R3F 4,000 + Bloom (hero) | 2.712 | 0.904 |
| MC whip-pan, standard / hero | 0.647 / 0.653 | 0.216 / 0.218 |

swangle is 2.3–5× slower (MA-standard 1.99; MB-hero 5.036).

### Cost model

- box s/frame = slot value ÷ 3.
- Standard tier:
  - **S-lo** = 0.141 (bench, 50/50 2D and glow);
  - **S-hi** = 0.183 (mean of the MA, MB and MC standard tests).
- Hero tier: **H-lo** = 0.904 (MB-hero); **H-hi** = 1.038 (BenchR3F).
- Hours = frames × [(1−h)·S + h·H] / 3600, where h is the hero share.
- Frames at 4:10:07: 450,210 at 30 fps, 360,168 at 24 fps.
- Each band runs from (S-lo, H-lo) to (S-hi, H-hi).
- **+25 %** is the bench's allowance for encode, conform, re-renders and retries. The plan does not say whether the 24 h budget includes it.

### Projected final-render hours at 4:10:07

At 4:02:00, every value is 3.2 % lower.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 % | 17.7–22.9 | 22.1–28.7 | 14.1–18.4 | 17.7–22.9 |
| 5 % | 22.4–28.3 | 28.0–35.4 | 17.9–22.6 | 22.4–28.3 |

| 15 % | 32.0–39.0 | 40.0–48.7 | 25.6–31.2 | 32.0–39.0 |

- **Cross-check:** the bench basis (S-lo with H-hi) at 5 % and 30 fps gives 23.3 h. E-001 quotes 23.2 h.
- **Each extra 1 % of hero share** costs 0.90–1.12 box-h at 30 fps and 0.72–0.90 at 24 fps.
  - The critic quotes "about 3.2 box-hours" per 1 % on a 3 h film.
  - The bench definition gives 0.81 box-h, because s/frame is measured per slot and the box runs 3 slots.
  - The chair uses the bench definition (challengeable).
- **Largest hero share within 24 h:**

  | | Pure render | With +25 % |
  |---|---|---|
  | 30 fps | 1.0–6.7 % | ≤ 1.6 %; the worst case is over budget even at 0 % |
  | 24 fps | 6.6–12.9 % | 1.0–6.7 % |

- **P10 plates:** 20–40 unique 10 s loops cost 1.5–3.5 box-h at 30 fps (1.2–2.8 at 24 fps). This is spent before P12. The cost of compositing them as video is *n/m*.
- **Previews:** ≈ ⅛ of a final pass.

### Other evidence

- **PSNR, swiftshader vs swangle:** Bench2D 47.5 dB, BenchGlow 46.6, BenchR3F 84, MA 46.2. MB-hero is **24.9** (22–27 when dense), although the frames "look identical side by side" (lookdev `README.md`, finding 1).
- **angle:** one probe only, equal to swiftshader (26 s).
- **R3F bug:** with `@remotion/three` 4.0.534, the first frame in each tab renders blank. A warmup fix exists (finding 3).
- **CSS hero FX are almost free:** MA 0.398 vs 0.384; MC 0.647 vs 0.653. The critic finds them "not distinguishable at contact-sheet scale".
- **Particles hurt legibility:** at 4,000 points, F4-A-hero scored legibility **4**. The critic: "Hero must not mean denser particles."
- **Critic's hero plan:** 8–10 % on average, up to 15 % only in CH-00, CH-33 and 3–4 transitions.
- **Timing is set in frames** (`04` §4: −3, −2, −4…−6 frames). One frame lasts 33.3 ms at 30 fps and 41.7 ms at 24 fps. Worst-case rounding is 16.7 ms and 20.8 ms respectively.

## 2 · ADR-003 Look and typography

All candidates share JetBrains Mono and Plex Sans Arabic 500–600 labels, and all are OFL.

| id | Arabic display | Latin | Look mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T-A** | Alexandria 700 at ≥ 56 px (Plex 600 below that; word-spacing 0.08 em) | Inter Tight 700 | 7.50 (hero 7.55) |
| **T-B** | Readex Pro 700 | Space Grotesk 700 | 7.40 |
| **T-C** | Plex Sans Arabic 700 | Inter Tight 700 | 7.15 |

- **Margins:** A leads B by 0.10 (1.4 %) and C by 0.35 (4.9 %). The scores are provisional and will be re-scored after three preview chapters.
- **Critic's ranking: A > B > C.**
  - B only "if the Arabic-typographer finds ر/ي collisions in A at real script".
  - "Do not mix B and A within a chapter."
  - In C, the impact word is "visibly lighter and smaller".
- **Shaping:** 0 errors. There is one tashkeel collision (F5, ثوانٍ), and it comes from the layout.
- **The G6a failure is driven by composition, depth and micro-legibility:**
  - F3 parity is 5;
  - F4 legibility is 4–5;
  - labels are about 11–12 px, while `04` §3.2 puts object labels at 28–40 px;
  - the critic proposes an 18 px floor, line-height ≥ 1.6 with diacritics, and +0.3 em between title and subtitle.
- **Best frames:** F6 8.00, F5 7.71.
- **Missing frames:** Holo-City, portal ignition, and an orbit with a kinetic word.

**Look baseline**

- **LK-F:** freeze the palette, tokens and pairing now. Critic fixes 1–5 become P7 requirements; G6a is re-scored after the fixes.
- **LK-H:** hold the freeze until a re-scored set (fixes 1–5 plus the 3 missing frames) reaches ≥ 8.0.

Also state the minimum label size: 18 px or 28 px.

Typography criteria: impact and look 0.30 · Arabic legibility and shaping safety 0.30 · parity 0.15 · pairing of digits, Latin and mono 0.10 · engineering risk 0.15.

Baseline criteria: G6a risk 0.35 · schedule and token risk 0.35 · reversibility 0.30.

## 3 · ADR-004 Captions

| id | Option |
|---|---|
| **CAP-AR** | Soft Arabic (`mov_text`, `ara`) + `.srt`/`.vtt` sidecars |
| **CAP-ARENT** | CAP-AR + soft English for the S1 trunk only (`00` §4 wording) |
| **CAP-AREN** | CAP-AR + soft English for the whole film |
| **CAP-BP** | Soft tracks + burned-in Arabic only in named low-intelligibility windows |
| **CAP-BF** | Full burned-in Arabic + soft tracks |

Screened out: no subtitle track at all (fails D10 and G10a).

**Evidence**

- **English text:** `gloss_en` exists for 3,126 of 3,126 sentences (`corpus/sentences/`). It is LLM-written and not fact-checked; accuracy *n/m*.
- **Arabic text:** G3 passed (S4 polished 27/27; 99.8 % of S5 words agree within 120 ms).
- **Plan guidance:** `04` §11 lists "Full burned-in captions" under *Don't*. Kinetic phrases are already ≤ 6 words and ≤ 2 lines (`04` §3.2).
- **S4 audio** (`corpus/audio/assets.json`): 48–64 kbps MP3, median −21.2 LUFS at source, median bandwidth 8.0 kHz (S1: 8.6 kHz). Intelligibility on the mix is *n/m* until G9a.
- **Not tested yet:** legibility, RTL `mov_text` in players, and sidecars when the direct link plays in a browser.
- **Fixability:** burned-in captions are tied to the render. Soft tracks can be fixed after P12 without re-rendering.

Criteria: accessibility and reach 0.25 · picture cleanliness and conflict with mandatory on-screen text 0.25 · accuracy exposure 0.15 · plan conformance 0.15 · cost and reversibility 0.10 · player compatibility 0.10.

## 4 · ADR-005 Music and SFX

| id | Music | SFX |
|---|---|---|
| **MS-P** | Procedural, synthesised in the repo (pads, pulses, risers, stingers) | Procedural (about 20 sounds, `03` P11) |
| **MS-C** | Procedural | CC0 + procedural |
| **MS-R** | Royalty-free library; terms and attribution logged | CC0 + procedural |
| **MS-S2** | S2 bed separated with Demucs | Procedural |

MS-S2 is screened out unless its provenance is found: its licence is unknown, and G9a requires "licenses logged".

**Evidence**

- **No music or SFX in the archives:** `assets.json` holds 71 speech assets only. S2's music_bed_score is 0.947 (S1: 0.0), from an unknown source (`reports/audio/sources.md`).
- **Silence and switches:** S1 has digital silence between phrases (floor −90 dB, 608 pauses). The S4 median floor is −87 dB. Voice switches: 66 in B100, 48 in B100-V2.
- **Mix targets:**
  - −16 ± 0.5 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1.0 dBTP;
  - music sidechain-ducked to about −24 to −28 LUFS-S under VO (`03` P11);
  - SFX at −22 to −36 LUFS-S, ≤ 30 cues/min, ≥ 350 ms apart, −6 dB under VO (`04` §9);
  - G9a: WER on the mix ≤ WER on VO alone + 2 points;
  - licences recorded in `docs/decisions/licenses.md`.
- **Tools:** ffmpeg and sox.
- **Network:** some hosts are blocked by the proxy (the DNSMOS/UTMOS weights were unreachable). Whether CC0 or library downloads are reachable is *n/m*.
- **Risk register:** R12 (`00` §8) names "owned/procedural or CC0".

Also state the bed level under VO: −24, −26 or −28 LUFS-S, or no bed under VO.

Criteria: licence safety 0.30 · premium feel 0.25 · VO intelligibility and mix compliance 0.20 · cost and schedule 0.15 · reversibility (re-mix from stems) 0.10.
